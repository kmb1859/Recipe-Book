Mild Chicken Taco Rice Bowls

Simple rice bowls with seasoned chicken, corn, carrots, and mild toppings.

4 servingsPrep/cook estimate: 30 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb chicken breast
  • 2 cups cooked white rice
  • 1 cup frozen corn
  • 1 cup frozen carrots
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • ½ tsp dried oregano
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ½ cup shredded cheddar
  • ½ cup plain Greek yogurt
  • Shredded lettuce (optional)
  • A small squeeze of lime (optional, if tolerated)

Instructions

  1. Season the chicken with paprika, oregano, and salt. Cook until done, then shred or chop.
  2. Heat the corn and carrots until hot.
  3. Divide the cooked rice among bowls. Add chicken and vegetables.
  4. Top with a little cheddar and Greek yogurt. Add lettuce or a small squeeze of lime if desired.

Cooking note: Follow safe food-handling practices and verify meat and poultry are fully cooked with a food thermometer.
