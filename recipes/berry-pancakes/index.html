Berry Pancakes

Small, tender pancakes made with low-FODMAP pancake mix and topped with fresh berries and maple syrup.

1 serving · 2–3 small pancakesAbout 10 minutes

Ingredients

  • ½ cup low-FODMAP pancake mix
  • Lactose-free milk, as needed to match the pancake mix package directions (the source quantity is obscured)
  • 1 egg white
  • ½ cup strawberries or blueberries
  • 1 tsp maple syrup
  • A little oil or cooking spray for the skillet

Instructions

  1. Whisk the pancake mix, egg white, and enough lactose-free milk to make batter according to the package-style ratio. The source recipe's exact milk quantity is obscured, so follow the mix package directions.
  2. Heat a lightly greased nonstick skillet over medium heat.
  3. Cook 2–3 small pancakes, turning when bubbles appear and the edges begin to set, until cooked through.
  4. Top with strawberries or blueberries and maple syrup.
