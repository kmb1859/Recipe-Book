Turkey Meatballs & Spaghetti

Baked turkey meatballs served with gluten-free spaghetti and low-FODMAP marinara.

4 servingsAbout 35 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb lean ground turkey
  • 1 egg
  • ½ cup gluten-free/low-FODMAP breadcrumbs
  • 2 tbsp grated Parmesan (optional)
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp black pepper
  • 12 oz gluten-free spaghetti
  • 24 oz low-FODMAP marinara
  • 1 tbsp olive oil

Instructions

  1. Heat oven to 400°F. Mix turkey, egg, breadcrumbs, Parmesan, seasoning, salt, and pepper. Form 20–24 small meatballs.
  2. Place on a lined baking sheet, brush lightly with oil, and bake 15–18 minutes, until the centers reach 165°F.
  3. Cook spaghetti according to package directions. Warm marinara and toss with pasta. Serve with meatballs.
