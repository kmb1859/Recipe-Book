Instant Pot Chicken & Gravy with Little Potatoes and Peas

Tender pressure-cooked chicken blended with savory gravy and served with Little Potato Co. Little Duos and peas.

4 servingsAbout 45 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 lb boneless, skinless chicken breasts, frozen
  • 1½ cups chicken broth
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ½ tsp dried parsley
  • ¼ tsp dried thyme (optional)
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1 tbsp cornstarch
  • 2 tbsp cold water
  • 2–4 tbsp lactose-free milk (optional, to adjust gravy consistency)
  • 1½ lb Little Potato Co. Little Duos
  • 1 cup peas
  • 1 cup water for cooking potatoes
  • 1 tbsp butter for potatoes (optional)

Instructions

  1. Place frozen chicken breasts in the Instant Pot with chicken broth, salt, parsley, optional thyme, and 1 tbsp butter. Cook on High Pressure for 15–20 minutes (use 20 minutes for thick breasts), followed by a 10-minute natural release. Check that chicken reaches 165°F.
  2. Remove chicken. Mix cornstarch with cold water. Turn the pot to Sauté and stir the slurry into the broth; simmer 1–2 minutes until thickened.
  3. Transfer chicken and thickened gravy to a blender. Blend briefly until smooth, following hot-liquid precautions. Add 2–4 tbsp milk if needed to adjust the consistency.
  4. Separately pressure-cook the Little Duos with 1 cup water on High Pressure for 5 minutes, then quick-release. Drain, reserving or discarding the cooking water. Add optional butter.
  5. Cook peas by microwaving or steaming until hot. Serve the potatoes and peas topped with the blended chicken-and-gravy mixture.
