Greek Yogurt Parfait

Lactose-free Greek yogurt layered with strawberries, blueberries, granola, and maple syrup.

1 serving5 minutes

Ingredients

  • ¾ cup lactose-free low-fat Greek yogurt
  • ½ cup strawberries
  • ¼ cup blueberries
  • 2 tbsp low-FODMAP granola
  • 1 tsp maple syrup

Instructions

  1. Layer the yogurt, strawberries, blueberries, and granola in a bowl or glass.
  2. Drizzle with maple syrup.
