Berry Crisp

A little baked berry crisp with an oat crumble topping.

1 servingAbout 25 minutes

Ingredients

  • ½ cup strawberries or blueberries
  • 2 tbsp rolled oats
  • 1 tsp brown sugar
  • 1 tsp flour or oat flour
  • 1 tsp butter

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 375°F.
  2. Place the berries in a small baking dish.
  3. Mix the oats, brown sugar, flour, and butter into crumbs and sprinkle over the berries.
  4. Bake for 15–20 minutes.
