Chicken Noodle Soup

A single-serving soup with cooked chicken, noodles, carrots, broth, and parsley.

1 servingAbout 15 minutes

Ingredients

  • 4 oz cooked chicken breast
  • 1 oz dry rice noodles or egg noodles
  • ½ cup carrots
  • ¼ cup celery, optional if tolerated
  • 2 cups low-FODMAP chicken broth
  • 1 tbsp parsley

Instructions

  1. Bring the broth to a simmer in a small saucepan.
  2. Add the carrots and noodles and cook until tender.
  3. Stir in the cooked chicken and heat through.
  4. Finish with parsley.
