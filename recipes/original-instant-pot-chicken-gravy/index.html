Original Instant Pot Chicken & Gravy with Potatoes and Peas

The earlier, simpler chicken-and-gravy dinner, made with chicken broth rather than the later apple-juice variation.

4 servingsOriginal recipe version

Ingredients

  • 1 lb boneless, skinless chicken breast
  • 1 cup chicken broth
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ½ tsp dried parsley
  • 1¼ lb potatoes, cut into chunks
  • ½ cup lactose-free milk
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1 cup peas
  • 1 tbsp cornstarch mixed with 2 tbsp cold water

Instructions

  1. Add the chicken, chicken broth, salt, and parsley to the Instant Pot.
  2. Cook on High Pressure for 10 minutes, followed by a 5-minute natural pressure release. Check that the chicken reaches 165°F.
  3. Remove the chicken and reserve about 1 cup of the cooking broth. Mix the cornstarch with 2 tbsp cold water, then whisk it into the reserved broth and simmer until thickened into gravy.
  4. Meanwhile, boil the potatoes until tender, about 15 minutes. Drain and mash with the lactose-free milk and butter.
  5. Heat the peas until tender.
  6. Serve the chicken with gravy, mashed potatoes, and peas.
