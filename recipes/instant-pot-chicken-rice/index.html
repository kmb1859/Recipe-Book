Instant Pot Chicken & Rice with Carrots

A simple one-pot meal with chicken, long-grain rice, carrots, and gentle herbs.

4 servingsPrep/cook estimate: 35 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb boneless, skinless chicken breasts or thighs
  • 1½ cups long-grain white rice
  • 3 cups low-sodium chicken broth
  • 2 cups frozen sliced carrots
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp dried thyme
  • ½ tsp dried parsley
  • Black pepper, to taste
  • A small amount of olive oil (optional)

Instructions

  1. Put the chicken in the Instant Pot. Add the broth, seasonings, and carrots.
  2. Rinse the rice well and spread it evenly over the top. Do not stir it deeply into the chicken.
  3. Cook on High Pressure for 5 minutes. Allow a 10-minute natural pressure release, then release the remaining pressure.
  4. Remove and shred the chicken, then stir everything together. If the rice is wet, let it rest uncovered for 5–10 minutes.

Cooking note: Follow safe food-handling practices and verify meat and poultry are fully cooked with a food thermometer.
