Strawberry Shortcake Cups

Easy individual desserts layered with angel food cake, strawberries, and whipped topping.

4 servingsPrep/cook estimate: 10 minutes

Ingredients

  • Angel food cake, cut into small pieces
  • Fresh strawberries, sliced
  • Low-fat whipped topping

Instructions

  1. Cut the angel food cake into small pieces.
  2. Layer cake pieces and sliced strawberries in individual cups.
  3. Finish with low-fat whipped topping and serve.

Cooking note: Follow safe food-handling practices and verify meat and poultry are fully cooked with a food thermometer.
