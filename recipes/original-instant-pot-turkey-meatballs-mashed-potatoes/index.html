Original Instant Pot Turkey Meatballs with Mashed Potatoes and Green Beans

The earlier homemade turkey meatball dinner with mashed potatoes and green beans, before later stovetop variations.

4 modest servingsAbout 30 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 lb lean ground turkey
  • ¼ cup plain breadcrumbs
  • 1 large egg
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ½ tsp dried parsley
  • ¼ tsp dried oregano
  • 1¼ lb potatoes
  • ½ cup milk
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • ¼ tsp salt (for the potatoes)
  • 2 cups green beans
  • 1 cup water (for pressure cooking)

Instructions

  1. Combine the ground turkey, breadcrumbs, egg, ½ tsp salt, parsley, and oregano. Form into approximately 12 meatballs.
  2. Place 1 cup water and the 1¼ lb potatoes in the Instant Pot. Add the meatballs for the recorded 8-minute High Pressure cook. The original notes do not preserve the exact rack or basket arrangement used to position the meatballs, so verify that setup before cooking.
  3. Cook on High Pressure for 8 minutes, then use a quick release. Check that the centers of the meatballs reach 165°F and that the potatoes are tender; if not, continue cooking safely until done.
  4. Mash the potatoes with the milk, butter, and ¼ tsp salt.
  5. Steam or microwave the green beans until tender, about 5–7 minutes, then serve with the meatballs and mashed potatoes.
