Mango & Strawberry Cups

Fresh mango and strawberries served chilled in individual cups.

4 servings10 minutes plus chilling

Ingredients

  • 2 mangoes, diced
  • 1 lb strawberries, sliced

Instructions

  1. Divide diced mango and sliced strawberries among four cups.
  2. Chill until serving.
