Breakfast Tacos

Corn tortillas filled with scrambled eggs, potatoes, tomato, cheddar, and mild salsa.

1 servingAbout 15 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 small corn tortillas
  • 1 egg
  • 2 egg whites
  • ½ cup diced cooked potato
  • 2 tbsp diced tomato
  • 1 tbsp shredded cheddar
  • 2 tbsp mild low-FODMAP salsa

Instructions

  1. Warm the tortillas.
  2. Scramble the egg and egg whites in a nonstick skillet.
  3. Warm the cooked potatoes.
  4. Assemble the tortillas with eggs, potatoes, tomato, cheese, and salsa.
