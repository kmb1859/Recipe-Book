Chicken Caprese Pasta

A simple pasta bowl with chicken, tomato, mozzarella, fresh basil, olive oil, and balsamic glaze.

1 servingAbout 20 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 oz dry pasta
  • 4 oz chicken breast, cooked and sliced
  • ½ cup diced tomato
  • 2 tbsp part-skim mozzarella
  • 1 tbsp fresh basil
  • 1 tsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp balsamic glaze
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Instructions

  1. Cook pasta according to package directions and drain.
  2. Toss warm pasta with cooked chicken, tomato, mozzarella, basil, and olive oil.
  3. Drizzle with balsamic glaze. Season lightly with salt and pepper.
