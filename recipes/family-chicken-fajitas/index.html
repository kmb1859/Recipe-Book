Family Chicken Fajitas

Sheet-pan chicken and bell peppers seasoned with cumin, paprika, chili, and lime.

4 servingsAbout 30 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2½ lb chicken breast, thinly sliced
  • 3 bell peppers, sliced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tsp cumin
  • 2 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp chili powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • Juice of 1 lime
  • 8 small tortillas
  • Optional cheese, lettuce, tomato, sour cream, or salsa

Instructions

  1. Heat oven to 425°F. Toss chicken and peppers with oil and seasonings.
  2. Spread on a sheet pan and roast 18–22 minutes, stirring once, until chicken reaches 165°F. Alternatively, cook in a large skillet.
  3. Finish with lime juice. Serve with warm tortillas and optional toppings.
