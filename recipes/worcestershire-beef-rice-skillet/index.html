Savory Worcestershire Beef and Rice Skillet

A mild, savory beef-and-rice skillet with peas and carrots in a flavorful, lightly thickened gravy.

4 generous portions25–30 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 lb lean ground beef
  • 2 microwave packages of plain white rice (about 8–10 oz each)
  • 1 steamable package of peas and carrots (approximately 10–12 oz)
  • 2½ cups beef broth
  • 1–2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce, to taste
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1½ tbsp cornstarch
  • ½ tsp dried parsley
  • ¼ tsp dried thyme
  • ¼ tsp onion powder (optional, if tolerated)
  • Salt to taste
  • Up to ½ cup additional water, as needed

Instructions

  1. Microwave the steamable peas and carrots according to the package directions.
  2. Brown the ground beef in a large skillet over medium heat, breaking it into small pieces. Drain excess grease if needed.
  3. Add the beef broth, Worcestershire sauce, butter, parsley, thyme, optional onion powder, and a little salt. Bring to a gentle simmer.
  4. Mix the cornstarch with a few tablespoons of cold water until smooth. Stir it into the simmering broth and cook until lightly thickened.
  5. Microwave the rice according to package directions. Add the rice and cooked vegetables to the skillet and stir to combine.
  6. Add up to ½ cup water as needed to loosen the sauce and make the skillet moist and savory. Taste and adjust Worcestershire sauce and salt.

This is the modified Wednesday version using microwave rice and a steamable peas-and-carrots package. It replaces the earlier uncooked-rice skillet.
