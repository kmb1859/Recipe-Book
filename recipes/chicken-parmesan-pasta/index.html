Easy Chicken Parmesan Pasta with Frozen Zucchini

An easy stovetop pasta with chicken, frozen zucchini, marinara, mozzarella, and Parmesan.

4 servingsPrep/cook estimate: 30 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 lb chicken breast, cut into small pieces
  • 12 oz pasta
  • 1½ cups low-fat marinara sauce
  • 2 cups frozen zucchini
  • ½–1 cup shredded mozzarella
  • ¼ cup grated Parmesan
  • Italian seasoning, to taste

Instructions

  1. Cook the pasta according to package directions and drain.
  2. Sauté the chicken until cooked through.
  3. Add the frozen zucchini and cook until tender.
  4. Stir in the marinara and Italian seasoning, then add the drained pasta.
  5. Top with mozzarella and Parmesan. Cover for 2–3 minutes, until the cheese melts.
Variation: If tomato sauce is bothersome, the source recipe suggests using a mild low-fat Alfredo-style sauce or broth with Parmesan instead.

Cooking note: Follow safe food-handling practices and verify meat and poultry are fully cooked with a food thermometer.
