Creamy Chicken and Vegetable Pasta

A mild stovetop pasta with diced chicken, peas, and a light creamy parsley sauce.

4 modest servings25 minutes

Ingredients

  • 8 oz pasta
  • ¾ lb chicken breast, diced
  • 1 cup frozen peas
  • 1 cup low-fat milk
  • 1 tbsp cornstarch
  • 2 tbsp cold milk (to mix with cornstarch)
  • 1 tsp olive oil
  • ½ tsp dried parsley
  • Salt, to taste
  • Optional: ¼ cup shredded mozzarella

Instructions

  1. Cook the pasta according to package directions. Drain and set aside.
  2. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the diced chicken and cook, stirring, until the chicken reaches 165°F in the thickest pieces.
  3. Whisk the cornstarch into 2 tbsp cold milk until smooth. Add the remaining milk and parsley to the skillet. Stir in the slurry and cook gently, stirring, until the sauce thickens.
  4. Add the frozen peas and cook until hot and tender. Stir in the cooked pasta and optional mozzarella until combined and the cheese melts.
  5. Season with salt to taste. Omit the optional cheese or use a tolerated alternative for a dairy-sensitive serving.
