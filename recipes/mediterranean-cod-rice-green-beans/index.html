Mediterranean Cod with Rice & Green Beans

Lemon and herb baked cod served with white rice and green beans.

4 servingsAbout 35 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 lb cod fillets
  • 2 cups uncooked white rice
  • 1½ lb green beans
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • ¾ tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp black pepper
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • Optional feta for family servings

Instructions

  1. Heat oven to 400°F. Place cod in a lightly oiled baking dish. Drizzle with oil and lemon juice; season with oregano, paprika, salt, and pepper.
  2. Bake 12–18 minutes, until fish reaches 145°F and flakes easily.
  3. Cook rice and green beans. Finish cod with parsley. Add feta if desired.
