Rice Pudding

A small, creamy stovetop rice pudding with lactose-free milk, cinnamon, and vanilla.

1 servingAbout 10 minutes

Ingredients

  • ½ cup cooked white rice
  • ¾ cup lactose-free 1% milk
  • 1 tsp sugar or maple syrup
  • ¼ tsp cinnamon
  • ½ tsp vanilla

Instructions

  1. Combine the cooked rice and milk in a small saucepan.
  2. Simmer over low heat for 8–10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until creamy.
  3. Stir in the sweetener, cinnamon, and vanilla.
