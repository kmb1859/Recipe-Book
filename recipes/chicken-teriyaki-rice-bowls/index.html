Chicken Teriyaki Rice Bowls

Chicken and tender vegetables in teriyaki sauce, served over white rice.

4 servingsAbout 35 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2½ lb chicken breast, cubed
  • 3 cups uncooked white rice
  • 1½ cups low-FODMAP teriyaki sauce
  • 2 zucchini, sliced
  • 2 carrots, thinly sliced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp cornstarch + 2 tbsp water (optional)

Instructions

  1. Cook rice according to package directions.
  2. Heat oil in a large skillet. Cook chicken until browned and the center reaches 165°F.
  3. Add zucchini and carrots; cook 4–6 minutes, until tender. Add teriyaki sauce.
  4. If a thicker sauce is desired, stir together cornstarch and water, add to the skillet, and simmer 1–2 minutes. Serve over rice.
