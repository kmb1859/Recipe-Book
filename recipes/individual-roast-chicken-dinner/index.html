Roast Chicken Dinner

A simple single-serving roast chicken dinner with potato, carrots, green beans, and gravy.

1 servingAbout 25–35 minutes

Ingredients

  • 5 oz skinless chicken breast or thigh
  • 1 medium potato
  • ½ cup carrots
  • ½ cup green beans
  • 1 tsp olive oil
  • ¼ cup low-FODMAP gravy

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 400°F.
  2. Toss the potato and carrots with the olive oil.
  3. Add the chicken and roast until the chicken reaches 165°F and the vegetables are tender, about 25–35 minutes.
  4. Steam the green beans.
  5. Warm the gravy separately and serve with the chicken and vegetables.
