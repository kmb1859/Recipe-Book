Original Mild Beef and Rice Skillet

The earlier one-pan version with uncooked rice, ground beef, carrots, and peas, before it was changed to microwave rice, steamable vegetables, and a Worcestershire broth sauce.

4 servingsOriginal recipe version

Ingredients

  • ½ lb ground beef
  • ¾ cup uncooked white rice
  • 1¾ cups water or broth
  • 1 cup diced carrots
  • ½ cup peas
  • ½ tsp dried parsley
  • Salt, to taste

Instructions

  1. Brown the ground beef in a large skillet over medium heat, breaking it into small pieces. Drain excess grease if needed.
  2. Stir in the uncooked rice, water or broth, diced carrots, parsley, and a little salt.
  3. Bring to a gentle boil, then cover and reduce the heat to low. Simmer until the rice is tender and the liquid is absorbed, following the rice package's cooking time and adding a little water if needed.
  4. Stir in the peas for the last few minutes of cooking, until heated through.
  5. Check that the rice and carrots are tender, then serve.
