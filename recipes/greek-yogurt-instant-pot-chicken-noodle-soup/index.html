Creamy Greek-Yogurt Instant Pot Chicken Noodle Soup (Modified Version)

A thicker, creamy variation of the family Instant Pot chicken noodle soup, finished with lactose-free Greek yogurt.

About 6 servingsAbout 45 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb chicken breast, frozen
  • 5 cups low-sodium chicken broth
  • 2 cups frozen carrots
  • 1 cup frozen green beans
  • 6 oz egg noodles
  • ¾–1 cup lactose-free low-fat Greek yogurt
  • 2 tbsp cornstarch
  • 2 tbsp cold water
  • Salt, to taste
  • Dried parsley and thyme, to taste

Instructions

  1. Add the chicken, broth, carrots, green beans, salt, parsley, and thyme to the Instant Pot. If the frozen chicken breasts are stuck together in a thick mass, separate or partially thaw them enough to cook evenly; the prior adjustment for thick or stuck-together frozen pieces was 17–18 minutes on High Pressure.
  2. Cook on High Pressure for 15 minutes (or 17–18 minutes for thick frozen pieces that remain together), followed by a 10-minute natural release. Check that the chicken reaches 165°F. Remove and shred the chicken.
  3. Switch to Sauté. Add the egg noodles and cook for 5–7 minutes, stirring as needed, until tender.
  4. Whisk the cornstarch with 2 tbsp cold water. Stir it into the soup and simmer briefly until slightly thickened.
  5. Turn off the heat. In a separate bowl, temper the Greek yogurt by gradually whisking in about 1 cup of hot broth. Stir the warmed yogurt mixture into the soup; do not boil after adding the yogurt.
  6. Return the shredded chicken to the pot and let the soup rest for 5–10 minutes before serving. Use up to 1 cup Greek yogurt for a creamier finish; the earlier note suggests 3 tbsp cornstarch if a thicker soup is desired.

Modified Greek-yogurt version, distinct from the standard Instant Pot Chicken Noodle Soup already listed on the site. The original modified conversation mentions frozen chicken and 15 minutes High Pressure plus 10 minutes natural release; a later adjustment for thick/stuck-together frozen pieces says 17–18 minutes.
