Apple-Glazed Chicken with Gravy

Instant Pot chicken gently cooked in apple juice and broth, served with gravy, mashed potatoes, and peas.

4 servingsAbout 35 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 lb chicken breasts
  • ¾ cup 100% apple juice
  • ¾ cup low-sodium chicken broth
  • 1 tbsp cornstarch
  • ½ tsp dried thyme
  • ½ tsp dried parsley
  • ⅛ tsp ground ginger (optional)
  • 1 tsp butter (optional)
  • 1½ lb potatoes
  • ½ cup milk
  • 1½ cups peas
  • 1 tbsp butter for the potatoes (optional)

Instructions

  1. Add the chicken, apple juice, chicken broth, thyme, parsley, and optional ginger to the Instant Pot.
  2. Cook on High Pressure for 8 minutes, followed by a 5-minute natural pressure release. Check that the thickest part of the chicken reaches 165°F.
  3. Meanwhile, cook the potatoes until tender; drain and mash with the milk and optional butter.
  4. Remove the chicken and reserve the cooking liquid. Mix the cornstarch with a little cold water until smooth. Bring the cooking liquid to a simmer and whisk in the slurry until thickened into gravy.
  5. Serve the chicken and gravy with mashed potatoes and peas.

This is the modified apple-juice version, replacing the earlier plain chicken-and-gravy recipe.
