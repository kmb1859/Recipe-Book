Steakhouse-Style Turkey Burgers

Seasoned turkey burgers with roasted potato wedges.

4 servingsAbout 40 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 lb lean ground turkey
  • 1 egg
  • ½ cup gluten-free breadcrumbs
  • 1 tsp suitable Worcestershire-style sauce
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 4 gluten-free/low-FODMAP burger buns
  • 4 cheese slices (optional)
  • Lettuce, tomato, and pickles as tolerated
  • 1½ lb potatoes
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • ½ tsp paprika
  • ½ tsp salt

Instructions

  1. Heat oven to 425°F. Cut potatoes into wedges, toss with olive oil, paprika, and salt; roast 30–35 minutes, turning once.
  2. Mix turkey, egg, breadcrumbs, Worcestershire-style sauce, mustard, salt, and pepper. Form 4 patties.
  3. Cook burgers in a skillet or on a grill until browned and 165°F inside, about 5–7 minutes per side depending on thickness.
  4. Add cheese during the final minute if using. Serve on buns with tolerated toppings and roasted potato wedges.
