Ginger-Soy Chicken with Rice & Vegetables

Ginger, maple, and soy-seasoned chicken with carrots and zucchini, served over rice. Includes a pressure-cooker method for mostly frozen chicken breasts.

4 servingsAbout 40 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2½ lb boneless, skinless chicken breasts, mostly frozen
  • 1 cup low-sodium chicken broth
  • ⅓ cup low-sodium tamari or soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp maple syrup
  • 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 2 cups uncooked white rice
  • 1 cup water for pot-in-pot rice
  • Carrots, sliced
  • Zucchini, sliced
  • 1 tbsp cornstarch + 2 tbsp water (optional, for thickening)

Instructions

  1. Whisk together broth, tamari or soy sauce, maple syrup, ginger, and rice vinegar. Pour into the Instant Pot and add the mostly frozen chicken breasts.
  2. Place rinsed rice and 1 cup water in a separate heat-safe bowl on a trivet above the chicken mixture (pot-in-pot).
  3. Cook on High Pressure for 12–15 minutes, followed by a 5–10-minute natural release. Check the thickest chicken pieces reach 165°F. If the chicken breasts are frozen individually and separated rather than in a solid mass, they may cook more quickly; check temperature before serving.
  4. Remove chicken and rice. Add sliced carrots to the sauce if they need further cooking; use Sauté as needed. Add zucchini and cook on Sauté for 3–5 minutes until tender.
  5. For a thicker sauce, stir together cornstarch and water, add to the sauce, and simmer until thickened. Slice or shred chicken and serve with rice and vegetables, spooning sauce over the top.
