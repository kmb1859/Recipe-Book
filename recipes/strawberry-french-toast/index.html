Strawberry French Toast

Golden skillet French toast topped with fresh strawberries and a little maple syrup.

1 servingAbout 15 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 slices sourdough bread
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 egg white
  • ¼ cup lactose-free milk
  • ½ tsp cinnamon
  • 1 cup sliced strawberries
  • 1 tsp maple syrup
  • Nonstick spray or ½ tsp oil

Instructions

  1. Whisk the egg, egg white, milk, and cinnamon in a shallow bowl.
  2. Dip the bread briefly on both sides in the egg mixture.
  3. Cook in a nonstick skillet over medium heat for about 2–3 minutes per side.
  4. Top with strawberries and maple syrup.
