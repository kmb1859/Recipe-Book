Cinnamon Baked Apples

Warm baked apples with cinnamon, a little brown sugar, and a splash of water.

4 servingsPrep/cook estimate: 35 minutes

Ingredients

  • 4 apples
  • Cinnamon, to taste
  • 1–2 tsp brown sugar
  • A splash of water

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 375°F.
  2. Slice the apples and place them in a baking dish.
  3. Sprinkle with cinnamon and brown sugar, then add a splash of water.
  4. Bake for 25–30 minutes, until tender.

Cooking note: Follow safe food-handling practices and verify meat and poultry are fully cooked with a food thermometer.
