Strawberries & Vanilla Yogurt

A quick dessert of sliced strawberries with lactose-free vanilla yogurt.

4 servings5 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb strawberries, sliced
  • 3 cups lactose-free vanilla yogurt

Instructions

  1. Divide yogurt among four bowls and top with sliced strawberries.
