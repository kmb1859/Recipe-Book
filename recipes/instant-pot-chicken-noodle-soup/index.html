Instant Pot Chicken Noodle Soup with Frozen Vegetables

Comforting chicken noodle soup made with broth, frozen vegetables, and egg noodles.

4 servingsPrep/cook estimate: About 45 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb chicken breasts
  • 6 cups low-sodium chicken broth
  • 2 cups frozen carrots
  • 1 cup frozen green beans
  • 1 tsp dried parsley
  • ½ tsp dried thyme
  • Salt, to taste
  • 6 oz egg noodles

Instructions

  1. Add the chicken, broth, carrots, green beans, parsley, thyme, and salt to the Instant Pot.
  2. Cook on High Pressure for 10 minutes. Allow a 10-minute natural pressure release.
  3. Remove the chicken and shred it.
  4. Turn the pot to Sauté, add the noodles, and cook for 5–7 minutes or until tender.
  5. Return the shredded chicken to the pot and stir.

Cooking note: Follow safe food-handling practices and verify meat and poultry are fully cooked with a food thermometer.
