Instant Pot Beef & Potato Stew

A hearty pressure-cooker stew with beef, baby potatoes, carrots, and thyme.

4 servingsPrep/cook estimate: About 1 hour

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb lean beef stew meat
  • 1½ lb baby potatoes, halved
  • 2 cups baby carrots
  • 3 cups low-sodium beef broth
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 1 tsp dried parsley
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp cornstarch
  • 2 tbsp cold water

Instructions

  1. Add the beef, potatoes, carrots, broth, thyme, parsley, and salt to the Instant Pot.
  2. Cook on High Pressure for 25 minutes. Allow a 10–15-minute natural pressure release.
  3. Mix the cornstarch with the cold water until smooth.
  4. Turn the pot to Sauté and stir in the slurry. Cook for a few minutes, until the broth thickens slightly.

Cooking note: Follow safe food-handling practices and verify meat and poultry are fully cooked with a food thermometer.
