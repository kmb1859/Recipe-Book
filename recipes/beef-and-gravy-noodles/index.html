Beef and Gravy Noodles

A simple stovetop dinner of ground beef, egg noodles, carrots, and thyme gravy.

4 modest servings25 minutes

Ingredients

  • ½ lb lean ground beef
  • 6 oz egg noodles
  • 1½ cups low-sodium beef broth
  • 1 tbsp cornstarch
  • 2 tbsp cold water (for the cornstarch slurry)
  • ¼ tsp dried thyme
  • 1 cup frozen carrots
  • Salt, to taste

Instructions

  1. Cook the egg noodles according to package directions. Drain and set aside.
  2. Brown the ground beef in a skillet over medium heat, breaking it into small pieces. Drain excess fat if needed.
  3. Add the beef broth, thyme, frozen carrots, and salt. Simmer for 6–8 minutes, until the carrots are tender.
  4. Stir the cornstarch with 2 tbsp cold water until smooth. Stir the slurry into the skillet and simmer until the broth thickens into gravy.
  5. Serve the beef-and-carrot gravy over the cooked egg noodles.

Four modest portions; approximately 25 minutes total.
