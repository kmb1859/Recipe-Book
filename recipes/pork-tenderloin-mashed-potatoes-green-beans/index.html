Pork Tenderloin with Mashed Potatoes & Green Beans

A simple roast pork tenderloin dinner with potatoes and green beans.

4 servingsAbout 40–50 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1½–2 lb pork tenderloin
  • 1½ lb baby potatoes
  • 1 bag frozen green beans
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 1 tsp dried parsley
  • Salt, to taste
  • Instant mashed potatoes, optional shortcut

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F.
  2. Season the pork tenderloin with thyme, parsley, and salt. Place it on a sheet pan with the potatoes; drizzle the potatoes with olive oil.
  3. Roast about 25–35 minutes, depending on the thickness of the tenderloin, until the pork reaches 145°F followed by a 3-minute rest and the potatoes are tender. Check the pork with a food thermometer.
  4. Heat the frozen green beans separately according to package directions.
  5. Rest the pork for 5–10 minutes before slicing. Serve with potatoes and green beans. For an easier option, use prepared instant mashed potatoes.
