Simple Roast Chicken Dinner

A whole roast chicken with potatoes and carrots, served with green beans.

4 servingsAbout 1 hour 45 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 whole chicken, 4–5 lb
  • 1½ lb carrots, cut into chunks
  • 1½ lb potatoes, cut into chunks
  • 1 lb green beans
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1½ tsp dried thyme
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1½ tsp salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 1 lemon, quartered

Instructions

  1. Heat oven to 425°F. Pat chicken dry; rub with oil and seasonings. Add lemon to the cavity if desired.
  2. Toss potatoes and carrots with remaining oil and arrange around chicken.
  3. Roast about 1¼–1½ hours, until the thickest part of the breast and thigh reaches 165°F. Rest 10–15 minutes.
  4. Steam or roast green beans while the chicken rests. Serve with the roasted chicken, potatoes, and carrots.
