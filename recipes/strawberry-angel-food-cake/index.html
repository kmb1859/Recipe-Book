Strawberry Angel Food Cake

Prepared angel food cake topped with fresh strawberries and lactose-free vanilla yogurt.

4 servings10 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 prepared angel food cake
  • 1½ lb strawberries, sliced
  • 1 cup lactose-free vanilla yogurt

Instructions

  1. Slice the angel food cake into four servings.
  2. Top each serving with sliced strawberries and a dollop of vanilla yogurt.
