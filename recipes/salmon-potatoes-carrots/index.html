Salmon with Potatoes and Carrots

A straightforward salmon dinner with boiled potatoes and tender carrots.

4 modest servingsAbout 25 minutes

Ingredients

  • 12 oz salmon
  • 1 lb potatoes, cut into chunks
  • 2 cups frozen carrots
  • 1 tsp olive oil
  • ½ tsp dried parsley
  • Salt, to taste

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 400°F. Place the salmon in a lightly oiled baking dish, brush or drizzle with the olive oil, and season with parsley and salt.
  2. Bake the salmon for 12–15 minutes, depending on thickness, until it reaches 145°F in the center and flakes easily.
  3. Meanwhile, boil the potatoes in water for 15–20 minutes, until fork-tender. Drain.
  4. Steam or microwave the frozen carrots according to package directions until tender.
  5. Serve the salmon with the potatoes and carrots.

The original plan records the salmon bake time as 12–15 minutes and the potatoes as 15–20 minutes; adjust the salmon time for the thickness of the fillet.
