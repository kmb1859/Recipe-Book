Skillet-Steamed Turkey Meatballs

Tender turkey meatballs gently steamed in a covered skillet, with an optional quick gravy from the pan liquid.

About 4 servingsAbout 25 minutes

Ingredients

  • ¾ lb lean ground turkey
  • ¼ cup plain breadcrumbs
  • 1 large egg
  • ½ tsp dried parsley
  • Salt, to taste
  • ½–¾ cup water or low-sodium chicken broth
  • Optional gravy: 1 tsp cornstarch mixed with 1 tbsp cold water

Instructions

  1. Combine the ground turkey, breadcrumbs, egg, parsley, and salt. Mix just until combined.
  2. Shape into about 12 meatballs, approximately 1½ inches across.
  3. Pour ½ cup water or broth into a large skillet and bring to a gentle simmer. Add the meatballs in a single layer.
  4. Cover and cook over medium-low to low heat for 12–16 minutes, turning the meatballs halfway through. Add a little more liquid if the skillet begins to dry out.
  5. Check that the centers reach 165°F. Remove the meatballs.
  6. Optional gravy: If desired, whisk the cornstarch slurry into the remaining skillet liquid and simmer until thickened. Add a little more broth or water if needed.

This uses the later skillet-steaming modification, not the original oven-baked meatball method.
