Cinnamon Yogurt Dip with Fruit

A quick, lightly sweet vanilla-yogurt dip for fresh fruit.

About 4 servings5 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 cup vanilla Greek yogurt
  • ½ tsp cinnamon
  • 1 tsp maple syrup
  • Strawberries, blueberries, or banana, for serving

Instructions

  1. Stir the yogurt, cinnamon, and maple syrup together until smooth.
  2. Serve as a dip with fresh fruit.
