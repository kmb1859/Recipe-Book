Vietnamese-Inspired Chicken Noodle Bowl

Rice noodles and grilled chicken with crisp vegetables, fresh herbs, and a tangy lime-fish-sauce dressing.

1 servingAbout 20 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 oz dry rice noodles
  • 4 oz grilled chicken
  • ¼ cup shredded carrot
  • ¼ cup cucumber, sliced
  • ½ cup lettuce, shredded
  • 2 tbsp fresh herbs
  • 1 tbsp lime juice
  • 1 tsp fish sauce
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tsp water

Instructions

  1. Cook rice noodles according to package directions and rinse.
  2. Whisk lime juice, fish sauce, sugar, and water to make the dressing.
  3. Toss noodles with chicken, carrot, cucumber, and lettuce. Drizzle with dressing and top with fresh herbs.
