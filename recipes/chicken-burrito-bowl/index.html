Chicken Burrito Bowl

A simple rice bowl with grilled chicken, corn, lettuce, tomato, salsa, and lime.

1 servingAbout 10 minutes, using cooked rice and chicken

Ingredients

  • ¾ cup cooked white rice
  • 4 oz grilled chicken breast
  • ¼ cup corn
  • ½ cup lettuce
  • ¼ cup tomato
  • 1–2 tbsp salsa
  • 1 tbsp avocado, optional
  • 1 lime wedge

Instructions

  1. Layer the rice, chicken, corn, lettuce, and tomato in a bowl.
  2. Add salsa and a small amount of avocado if tolerated.
  3. Finish with a squeeze of lime.
