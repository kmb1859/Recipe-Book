Easy Chocolate Pudding Cups

Simple individual portions of chocolate pudding, chilled and ready to serve.

4 servingsAbout 10 minutes plus chilling

Ingredients

  • 1 package instant chocolate pudding mix
  • 2 cups low-fat milk

Instructions

  1. Prepare the pudding according to package directions, using 2 cups of low-fat milk.
  2. Divide among four small cups or containers.
  3. Refrigerate until set.
