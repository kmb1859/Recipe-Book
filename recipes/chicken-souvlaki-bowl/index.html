Chicken Souvlaki Bowl

Seasoned chicken over rice with cucumber, tomato, carrot, feta, and a simple lemon-dill yogurt sauce.

1 servingAbout 25 minutes

Ingredients

  • 4 oz chicken breast
  • ¾ cup cooked rice
  • ¼ cup cucumber, diced
  • ¼ cup tomato, diced
  • ¼ cup shredded carrot
  • 1 tbsp feta
  • 2 tbsp lactose-free yogurt
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • ½ tsp dried dill
  • ½ tsp olive oil
  • Oregano, salt, and pepper, to taste

Instructions

  1. Season chicken with oregano, salt, and pepper. Heat olive oil in a skillet and cook chicken until it reaches 165°F; slice.
  2. Mix yogurt, lemon juice, and dill to make a quick sauce.
  3. Serve chicken over rice with cucumber, tomato, carrot, and feta. Add the yogurt sauce.
