Warm Cinnamon Pears

Tender warm pear slices lightly sweetened and scented with cinnamon.

1 serving5 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 pear, sliced
  • ¼ tsp cinnamon
  • 1 tsp brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp water

Instructions

  1. Place the pear slices in a microwave-safe bowl.
  2. Add the cinnamon, brown sugar, and water.
  3. Cover loosely and microwave for 2–3 minutes, stirring once, until tender. Microwave a little longer if needed.
