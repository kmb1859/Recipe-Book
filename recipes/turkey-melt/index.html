Turkey Melt

A toasted sourdough sandwich with sliced turkey, tomato, Swiss cheese, and mustard.

1 servingAbout 10 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 slices sourdough bread
  • 3 oz sliced turkey breast
  • 1 tomato slice
  • ½ oz Swiss cheese
  • 1 tsp yellow mustard

Instructions

  1. Spread the mustard on the bread.
  2. Add the turkey, tomato, and cheese.
  3. Toast in a skillet or sandwich press until the bread is crisp and the cheese melts.
