Banana Oatmeal

Warm oatmeal made with milk or water and topped with banana, cinnamon, and maple syrup.

1 servingAbout 5 minutes

Ingredients

  • ½ cup rolled oats
  • 1 cup lactose-free skim or 1% milk, or water
  • ½ firm banana, sliced
  • 1 tsp maple syrup
  • ¼ tsp cinnamon

Instructions

  1. Combine the oats and milk or water in a microwave-safe bowl.
  2. Microwave for 1½–2 minutes and stir.
  3. Add the banana, cinnamon, and maple syrup.
