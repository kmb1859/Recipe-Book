Loaded Breakfast Potato

A baked potato topped with turkey sausage, scrambled eggs, cheddar, and chives.

1 servingAbout 10 minutes, using a baked potato

Ingredients

  • 1 medium baked potato
  • 1 egg
  • 2 egg whites
  • 1 oz lean turkey sausage
  • 1 tbsp shredded cheddar
  • 1 tbsp chopped chives or green onion tops

Instructions

  1. Split the hot baked potato.
  2. Cook the turkey sausage in a nonstick skillet.
  3. Scramble the egg and egg whites.
  4. Pile the sausage and eggs onto the potato and top with cheese and chives.
