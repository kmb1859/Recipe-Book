Original Oven-Baked Turkey Meatballs with Potatoes & Green Beans

The original sheet-pan-style turkey meatball dinner, with roasted potatoes and green beans on the side, before the meatballs were changed to a covered-skillet method.

4 modest servingsOriginal recipe version

Ingredients

  • ¾ lb lean ground turkey
  • ¼ cup plain breadcrumbs
  • 1 large egg
  • ½ tsp dried parsley
  • Salt, to taste
  • 1 lb potatoes, cubed
  • 2 cups frozen green beans
  • 1 tbsp olive oil

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 400°F.
  2. Toss the cubed potatoes with about half the olive oil and a little salt. Spread on a baking sheet and bake for 10 minutes.
  3. Meanwhile, combine the ground turkey, breadcrumbs, egg, parsley, and salt. Mix just until combined and shape into about 12 meatballs.
  4. Add the meatballs to the baking sheet with the potatoes. Bake for 18–22 minutes, until the meatballs reach 165°F and the potatoes are tender.
  5. Steam the green beans separately until tender, about 5–7 minutes. Serve with the meatballs and potatoes.
