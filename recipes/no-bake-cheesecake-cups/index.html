No-Bake Cheesecake Cups

Easy no-bake cheesecake-style cups with a creamy yogurt and cream-cheese filling, graham-cracker crumbs, and berries.

About 4 servings15 minutes plus chilling

Ingredients

  • 4 oz light cream cheese, softened
  • ½ cup plain Greek yogurt
  • 2 tbsp powdered sugar
  • ½ tsp vanilla extract
  • Crushed graham crackers, for the base
  • Fresh berries, for topping

Instructions

  1. Beat or stir the softened cream cheese until smooth.
  2. Mix in the Greek yogurt, powdered sugar, and vanilla until creamy.
  3. Divide crushed graham crackers among small serving cups.
  4. Spoon the cheesecake mixture over the crumbs and top with berries.
  5. Refrigerate until chilled.
