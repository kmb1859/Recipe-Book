Dinner

Sheet-Pan Meatballs, Potatoes & Carrots

An easy sheet-pan dinner with roasted baby potatoes, carrots, and ready-made meatballs.

4 servingsAbout 40 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1–1½ lb lean frozen or refrigerated meatballs
  • 1½ lb baby potatoes, halved
  • 1 bag baby carrots
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt, to taste
  • Italian seasoning, to taste

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F.
  2. Toss the potatoes and carrots with olive oil, salt, and Italian seasoning.
  3. Spread the vegetables on a sheet pan and roast for 20 minutes.
  4. Add the meatballs to the pan and roast another 15–20 minutes, until the meatballs are heated through and the vegetables are tender. Follow package directions for frozen meatballs.
