Vanilla Pudding with Berries

Classic chilled vanilla pudding served with fresh berries.

4 servingsAbout 10 minutes plus chilling

Ingredients

  • 1 package instant vanilla pudding mix
  • 2 cups low-fat milk
  • Fresh berries, for serving

Instructions

  1. Prepare the pudding according to package directions, using 2 cups of low-fat milk.
  2. Refrigerate until set.
  3. Spoon into four portions and serve with berries.
