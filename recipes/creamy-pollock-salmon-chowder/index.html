Creamy Pollock & Salmon Chowder

A hearty, creamy Instant Pot chowder with pollock, canned salmon, potatoes, peas, carrots, and corn.

About 6–8 servingsAbout 40 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 lb frozen pollock fillets
  • 1 can salmon (about 14.75 oz), drained and checked for bones
  • 3 cups frozen diced potatoes
  • 2 cups frozen peas and carrots
  • 1½ cups frozen corn
  • 5 cups low-sodium chicken or vegetable broth
  • 2 cups 1% milk
  • ¾ cup low-fat lactose-free Greek yogurt
  • 3 tbsp cornstarch
  • 1 tsp dried parsley
  • ½ tsp dried thyme
  • ¾ tsp salt
  • Optional: 1 tbsp butter and black pepper

Instructions

  1. Add the frozen diced potatoes, peas and carrots, corn, broth, parsley, thyme, salt, and frozen pollock to the Instant Pot.
  2. Cook on High Pressure for 4 minutes. Allow a 5-minute natural release, then carefully release remaining pressure.
  3. Remove the pollock and gently flake it into bite-sized pieces. Set aside.
  4. Whisk the cornstarch into the milk until smooth. Set the Instant Pot to low Sauté and gradually stir in the milk mixture. Simmer for 3–5 minutes, stirring, until the chowder thickens.
  5. Turn off the heat. Temper the Greek yogurt by stirring a little hot chowder into it, then stir the warmed yogurt mixture into the pot.
  6. Fold in the flaked pollock and drained canned salmon. Add optional butter and black pepper. Warm gently without boiling and serve.

This is the later full-size Instant Pot pollock-and-salmon version. The earlier half-size stovetop salmon chowder is superseded for this recipe thread.
