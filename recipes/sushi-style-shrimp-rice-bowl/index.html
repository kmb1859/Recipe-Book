Sushi-Style Shrimp Rice Bowl

A quick rice bowl topped with cooked shrimp, cucumber, carrot, soy sauce, and sesame seeds.

1 serving10 minutes

Ingredients

  • ¾ cup cooked sushi or white rice
  • 4 oz cooked shrimp
  • ¼ cup cucumber, diced
  • ¼ cup shredded carrot
  • 1 tbsp avocado (optional)
  • 1 tsp low-sodium soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame seeds

Instructions

  1. Place rice in a bowl.
  2. Top with shrimp, cucumber, carrot, and optional avocado.
  3. Drizzle with soy sauce and sprinkle with sesame seeds.
