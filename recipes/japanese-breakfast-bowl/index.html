Japanese Breakfast Bowl

A savory rice bowl with egg, chicken, carrot, tamari, sesame, and green onion tops.

1 servingAbout 10 minutes, using cooked rice and chicken

Ingredients

  • ¾ cup cooked white rice
  • 1 egg
  • 2 egg whites
  • 2 oz grilled chicken
  • ¼ cup cooked carrot
  • 1 tsp tamari
  • 1 tsp sesame seeds
  • 1 tbsp green onion tops

Instructions

  1. Warm the rice and chicken.
  2. Scramble the egg and egg whites.
  3. Assemble the bowl with rice, chicken, eggs, and carrot.
  4. Add tamari, sesame seeds, and green onion tops.
