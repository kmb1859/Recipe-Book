Creamy Swedish-Style Turkey Meatballs with Potatoes and Carrots

A mild one-skillet meal with frozen turkey meatballs, potatoes, carrots, and creamy parsley-thyme gravy.

4 servingsAbout 25 minutes

Ingredients

  • 20–24 frozen Simek turkey meatballs
  • 3–4 leftover baked potatoes, cut into chunks
  • 2 cups baby carrots
  • 1¼ cups broth
  • ½ cup lactose-free milk
  • ½ cup lactose-free Greek yogurt
  • 1 tbsp cornstarch
  • 2 tbsp cold water
  • ½ tsp dried parsley
  • ¼ tsp dried thyme

Instructions

  1. Add the baby carrots and broth to a large skillet. Cover and simmer for 6–8 minutes to begin softening the carrots.
  2. Add the potato chunks and frozen meatballs to the same skillet. Keep the carrots and potatoes in the skillet; add the meatballs and heat them in the broth according to the package directions, covered, until hot throughout.
  3. Whisk the cornstarch with 2 tbsp cold water until smooth. Stir it into the skillet and simmer for 2–3 minutes, until the broth thickens.
  4. Stir in the lactose-free milk and heat gently for 1–2 minutes. Do not let the mixture boil vigorously.
  5. In a small bowl, temper the Greek yogurt with a spoonful or two of the hot sauce, then stir it into the skillet off the heat. Do not boil after adding the yogurt.
  6. Stir in the parsley and thyme and serve.

The original conversation specifies that the carrots and potatoes remain in the skillet throughout and that the meatballs cook with the broth; the gravy is thickened before the milk and yogurt are added. Follow the meatball package directions for heating time.
